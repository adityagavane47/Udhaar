Every business in India runs on udhaar. Now it settles itself.

Suppliers wait months for big buyers to pay. Udhaar turns a buyer-confirmed invoice into a token that investors fund today, that suppliers can pass down the chain, and that repays every holder automatically on the due date.

Who waits

The money stops one step too early.

India's TReDS platforms finance the supplier who sells to the big company. The smaller firms who supply him still wait. Ramesh can hand Suresh part of the invoice token instead of cash, and BigBuild's payment reaches Suresh directly.

FOLIO 01

Write it

The invoice is uploaded and its fingerprint goes into a public registry. The same invoice can't be financed twice.

FOLIO 02

Sign it

The buyer confirms the debt with a message signed by its own Hedera account. No signature, no listing.

FOLIO 03

Split it

It becomes 500 tokens that only verified wallets can hold. Investors buy them, or Ramesh pays his suppliers with them.

FOLIO 04

Settle it

The buyer pays once. Every holder's tokens are burned and paid at full face value, automatically.
